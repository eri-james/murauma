About MURA

MURA (Malayan Umamusume Racing Association) is a club and a community for Umamusume: Pretty Derby players from Malaysia and Singapore. We get together to share our love for the game through weekly race events, sharing game info, and simply celebrating Umamusume with camaraderie.
