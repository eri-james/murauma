MURA Website Launched!

Published on: June 30, 2025

Welcome, trainers, to the brand new official community hub for the Malayan Umamusume Racing Association (MURA)! We are thrilled to launch this platform as a central place for all our members from Malaysia and Singapore to connect, share, and celebrate our love for Umamusume: Pretty Derby.

What to Expect

This website will be your go-to source for everything MURA. Here’s a quick rundown of what you can find:

  • News & Announcements: All official news, from event results to community updates, will be posted here.
  • Event Details: Find information on our major events like the Merdeka Cup and our regular weekly races.
  • Community Hub: A portal to access game guides, member-created content, and our official member list.

A Word from the Admins

We've been planning this for a while and are excited to finally share it with you all. Our goal is to create a more organized and engaging space for the community outside of Discord. We hope you'll find it useful and fun!

This is just the beginning. We have many more features planned for the future, so stay tuned. As always, thank you for being a part of MURA. Let's continue to enjoy raising the best horse girls together!
