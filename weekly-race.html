Weekly Race: Mile Championship

Get your best milers ready for a showdown!

This week, we're heading to the Yasuda Kinen (Tokyo Racecourse) for a classic test of speed and strategy in the Mile Championship. It's time to see who has trained the ultimate miler!

Race Details

  • Race Yasuda Kinen
  • Track Tokyo Racecourse
  • Distance 1600m (Turf) (Left)
  • Conditions Great
  • Deadline Saturday, 11:59 PM

How to Participate

Joining is simple! The room will be opened on Saturday evening. Please keep an eye on the #♣️-club-announcement channel in our Discord for the Room ID and password. Simply join the room in-game with your best miler before the deadline.

Good luck to all participating trainers!
