U.A.F. Scenario: A Complete Guide

By: TrainerAce | June 28, 2025

The 'Unite and Fight' (U.A.F.) scenario is all about teamwork and strategic resource management. Unlike previous scenarios, you'll be managing the condition of an entire team, not just your main Umamusume.

Key Mechanics

  • Team Morale: Keeping this high is crucial for bonus training effects. This is affected by your race results and specific event choices.
  • Rival Battles: Winning these provides massive stat boosts and rare skill hints. Prioritize these when they appear!
  • Final Relay: The final evaluation is a team-based relay race. A balanced team is more important than a single superstar. Ensure you have strong runners for Short, Mile, Medium, and Long distances.

Recommended Support Cards

Focus on cards that provide AoE (Area of Effect) buffs or those with skills that activate in the final leg of a race. SSR Kitasan Black remains a top-tier choice, but SRs like Eishin Flash (Stamina) also perform exceptionally well here.
