Beginner's Guide to Support Card Selection

By: MURA Staff | June 15, 2025

Your choice of Support Cards is just as important as the Umamusume you're training. Here's a breakdown for new trainers.

Card Types

  • Speed: Essential for almost every build. Helps you stay at the front.
  • Stamina: Crucial for longer races (2000m+). Prevents your horse girl from running out of steam.
  • Power: Helps with acceleration and navigating through crowds. Important for all race types.
  • Guts: Affects last-spurt speed and stamina consumption when tired.
  • Intelligence: Helps your horse girl conserve stamina, choose better paths, and activate skills more reliably.
  • Friend: Provides unique events and powerful buffs.

Top F2P-Friendly SSR Cards

If you get these, prioritize leveling them up:

  • Kitasan Black (Speed): Widely considered the best card in the game. Provides excellent training bonuses and a powerful skill.
  • Super Creek (Stamina): Provides the powerful 'Maestro' recovery skill, which is a lifesaver in long-distance races.
  • Tazuna (Friend): Excellent for reducing failure rates, managing motivation, and providing decent stat boosts.
