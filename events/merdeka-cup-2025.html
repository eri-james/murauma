M.U.R.A. Triple Tiara

28 August 2025, Thursday @ 21:00

Eligibility: Only mares Umamusume.

Format: Send one Umamusume per race.

Races:
  • Oka Sho (Mile)
  • Oaks (Medium)
  • Shuuka Sho (Medium)

Scoring: Placement is determined by the average placement across all 3 races. A lower average equals a higher final placement.

Progression: Top 5 will move on to the finals.

Prize: Top 3 earn the M.U.R.A. Triple Tiara title (Discord Role).

M.U.R.A. Triple Crown

29 August 2025, Friday @ 21:00

Eligibility: Only stallions Umamusume.

Format: Send one Umamusume per race.

Races:
  • Satsuki Sho (Medium)
  • Japan Derby (Medium)
  • Kikkuka Sho (Long)

Scoring: Placement is determined by the average placement. A lower average equals a higher final placement.

Progression: Top 5 will move on to the finals. (If a participant already qualified from the Triple Tiara, the next participant in line will be counted).

Prize: Top 3 earn the M.U.R.A. Triple Crown title (Discord Role).
